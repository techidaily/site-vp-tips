## So, What's the Best Way to Record Powerpoint Presentation?

Usually you have two options to capture a slideshow with narration:

### Option 1: Record Narration over Presentation with Powerpoint itself.

This option based on you're the owner of the presentation, Powerpoint allows you to record presentation with audio and video. 

1\. After you've finished your presentation, click the "Slide Show" tab, and click "Record Slide Show".
