### Additional Information: WebM Video Codecs (VP9 and VP8)

[VP8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VP8): Developed by Google, VP8 is the successor to VP7\. It is an open & free video compression format and is served as a replacement for the Graphics Interchange Format (GIF). There are many browsers support playing VP8 video in HTML5 video, for instance, Opera, Firefox, Chrome, Chromium and more. According to the comparison between VP8 and H.264, people found that H.264 has a slight quality advantage.

VP9: Obviously, VP9 is a successor to VP8 and the main rival of High Efficiency Video Coding (HEVC/H.265). There are many [differences between H.265 and VP9](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/), for example, HEVC/H.265 has higher compression efficiency; H.265 video has a better visual quality on low bitrates than VP9; H.265 has shorter encoding time; VP9 has better compatibility with web browsers than HEVC, etc. You can choose your desired codec according to different requirements.
