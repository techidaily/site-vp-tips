**Instagram Detailed Video Specifications**

* _**Resolution:**_ Maximum width 1080 pixels (height anything, although within the same ratios as images)
* _**Frame Rate:**_ 29.96 frames per second (max 30fps, but should be fixed, not variable)
* _**Format:**_ H.264 codec / MP4
* _**Bitrate:**_ 5,500 kbps video bitrate (although Instagram may reduce this to 3,500kbps when we post it)
* _**Audio Codec:**_ AAC audio codec at 44.1 kHz mono (up to 128kbps)
* _**File Size:**_ Not specifically limited by Instagram, but we recommend keeping your videos under 50MB.
* _**Video Length:**_ Videos need to be at least 3 seconds and no more than 60 seconds long, or Instagram won't let us upload them
