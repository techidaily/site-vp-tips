###  Things to Note Before DVD Disc Duplication 

Before we copy a DVD to a blank DVD disc, we'd better have a look at the things below:

1\. When clone DVD to DVD, the very first step is to make a disc image of the original DVD movie. A disc image, usually in ISO format, contains the copy of the entire contents and structure of a DVD, or CD disk so that you can duplicate to DVD disc that is blank, the size of which equals to that of the data stored on the disc; 

2\. To backup DVD to another disc in 1:1 ratio, select a blank disc of the same size and type as that of the original one.

## DVD Duplication Part 2: How to Copy DVD to DVD Disc with Single DVD Drive 

For windows users, there are numerous DVD duplicators available for users to choose from. For Mac users, they may face with fewer choice. Fear not, the built-in Disk Utility is the ideal option to [burn DVD on Mac](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/). No extra software required. Here's [how to rip DVD on Mac](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/) and make DVD disc.

**Step 1**. Launch this top [DVD burner for Mac](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/): head to Applications > Utilities > Disk Utility.

**Step 2**. Insert a blank DVD or CD into the drive. If the ISO file is less than 4.38GB, a single layer DVD disc 5 is enough; if the ISO image is larger than 4.7GB, you will need a dual layer DVD, namely DVD 9.

**Step 3**. Drag and drop the iso file to the left pane in Disk Utility. Now both the blank disk and the .iso should will display in the Disk Utility.

**Step 4**. Select the .iso file you want to clone to DVD disc, and click on the Burn button. The Disk Utility will start the DVD disc duplication process automatically. After the DVD is duplicated, the disc will be ejected from the drive. If the DVD is protected, switch to [DVD decrypter Mac](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/).
