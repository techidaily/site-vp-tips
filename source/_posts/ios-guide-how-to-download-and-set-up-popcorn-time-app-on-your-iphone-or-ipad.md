###  How to Download and Install Popcorn Time iOS App on iPhone iPad without Jailbreak

![Download Popcorn Time iOS App](https://www.macxdvd.com/mac-dvd-video-converter-how-to/article-image/popcorn-time-ios.jpg) 

Download Popcorn Time app on iPhone

**Step 1**: Go to Settings > General > Date & Time > Turn off 'Set Automatically' and change the date to August 1, 2014\. 

**Step 2:** Open Safari, go to the [Popcorn Time iOS app download site](http://www.pgyer.com/popcorntime), and click on the green box to download it.

**Step 3**: Press the button "Install" to begin install Popcorn Time iOS for iPhone iPad.

**Step 4**: Set the date back to 'Set Automatically'. Hit 'Trust' on the pop-up window. Then the job is done! You can finally stream and watch Popcorn Time movies on your iPhone iPad without jailbreak.
