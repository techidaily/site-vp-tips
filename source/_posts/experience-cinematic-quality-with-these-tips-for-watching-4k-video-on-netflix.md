### Can I Stream 4K Titles on Netflix?

Of course, yes. But there are something you need prepare for your Netflix 4K movies and TV shows streaming:

* A 4K compatible device - it's currently limited to Roku 4's, Nvidia Shield's and TiVo Bolts some TV's are supported too.
* A 4K TV - Nowadays, most TVs are available for 3840×2160 4K videos playback. And Samsung, LG, Sony always the options for [best 4K TVs](https://shop-links.co/link/?exclusive=1&publisher_slug=itechdaily19598&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.macxdvd.com%2Fmac-dvd-video-converter-how-to%2Fbest-4k-tv-from-samsung-sony-lg.htm).
