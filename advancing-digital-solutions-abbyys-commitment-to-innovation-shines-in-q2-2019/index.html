ABBYY Invests in Technology Innovation During Q2 2019

July 19, 2019

Acquisition Plans and Enhancements to Solutions Increase Company’s Content IQ Capabilities

ABBYY focused on expanding and enhancing its solutions during the second quarter of 2019 (Q2 2019). Its Content IQ portfolio comprises of a set of enabling technologies that help digital workforces discover, understand and unlock value contained in enterprise content. The intent to acquire TimelinePI serves to add process intelligence capabilities (Process IQ) to the company‘s Content IQ capabilities. Additionally, advances made to mobile and cloud solutions were among the highlights during the quarter.

“We are focused on our strategy to deliver business value to our customers and partners through our integrated portfolio of AI-enabling technologies that increase digital workers‘ intelligence,“ commented Ulf Persson, CEO at ABBYY. “Investments in innovation is critical to this strategy. By adding Process IQ to our Content IQ offering, we will further augment the value we bring to our partners and customer as they realise their digital transformation initiatives.”

Here are some of the highlights from Q2 2019.

Expansion

ABBYY Announces Intent to Acquire TimelinePI to Deliver Digital Intelligence for Enterprise Processes

Philadelphia based TimelinePI provides a comprehensive process intelligence platform designed to empower users to understand, monitor and optimize any business process. The global process analytics market size is expected to grow to $1.4m by 2023 and the acquisition of TimelinePI is intended as a strategic investment by ABBYY into the emerging process intelligence market and strengthen its Digital IQ capabilties.

Partnership News

ABBYY and M-Files Strengthen Collaborative Partnership to Accelerate Enterprise Digital Business Transformations

ABBYY has become an M-Files Certified Application Partner (CAP) following the release of its FlexiCapture Connector for M-Files. The connector seamlessly integrates ABBYY’s intelligent data extraction and recognition capabilities with M-Files intelligent information management solution to fully automate end-to-end business solutions, like accounts payable invoice automation.

Product News

ABBYY Launches Mobile Web Capture SDK to Revolutionize Customer Onboarding on Mobile Devices

ABBYY Mobile Web Capture is a new SDK enabling developers to add real-time image and data capture to their mobile web-based onboarding scenarios. Suitable for iOS and Android, the SDK will help companies to dramatically accelerate onboarding processes, decrease client dropout rates caused by manual data entry, and create a better customer experience.

New ABBYY FineScanner AI brings the Power of Neural Networks to Document Scanning and Text Recognition

ABBYY FineScanner AI, is the next-generation mobile scanner for iOS and Android. The new AI-enabled functionality allows the user to instantly find photos of documents in the gallery and recognize the text in them without an Internet connection.

ABBYY FlexiCapture Cloud Accelerates Adoption and Processing with new REST API and Real-Time Capture

ABBYY announced a series of innovations to ABBYY FlexiCapture, an AI-enabled enterprise platform to automate document processing workflows and convert unstructured content into structured data for better business outcomes. The updates include the launch of the ABBYY FlexiCapture Cloud REST API (Representational State Transfer Application Programming Interface) and the introduction of the new Real-Time Capture technology for real-time document processing in the cloud.

Awards

ABBYY executives and solutions were honored with industry accolades for their vision, execution and market acceptance.

ABBYY Recognized Among Hot VendorsTM in Document Analytics, 2019 by Aragon Research

The research firm recognized ABBYY for its complete range of AI-based technologies and solutions transforming business documents and content into business value. The solutions highlighted by Aragon Research were ABBYY Vantage and FlexiCapture.

ABBYY Received AI Breakthrough Award for Vision – Best Image Processing Solution

AI Breakthrough, a leading market intelligence organization that recognizes top companies, technologies and products in the global AI market, recognized ABBYY FlexiCapture for the Vision – Best Image Processing Solution. The awards program attracted more than 2,500 nominations from 15 different countries and selected companies and solutions that drive innovation and exemplify the best in AI-related technology.

FeaturedCustomers Named ABBYY a Market Leader in the ‘Winter 2019 Accounts Payable Software Customer Success Report ’

FeaturedCustomers recognized ABBYY as a Market Leader in Accounts Payable Software for its substantial customer base, market share and customer success testimonials that validated its vision.

About ABBYY

ABBYY is a global leader in Content IQ technologies and solutions. ABBYY offers a complete range of AI-based technologies and solutions transforming business documents and content into business value. By providing digital transformation solutions to financial services, insurance, transportation, healthcare, and other industries, the company helps organizations achieve the next wave of growth by understanding customers and delivering responsive real-time intelligent systems. The flexibility of ABBYY AI solutions enables customers to utilize a diverse range of advanced technologies, platforms and solutions for classification, text analytics, data and entity extraction, and data validation via any communication channel and in any format. ABBYY technologies are used and licensed by some of the largest international enterprises and government organizations, as well as SMBs and individuals.
